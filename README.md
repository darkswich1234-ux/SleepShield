# 🌙 Sleep Shield

**Sleep Shield** is a dedicated sleep companion and productivity app designed to help you reclaim your nights. By creating a protective barrier around your most distracting apps during your sleep window, Sleep Shield ensures you get the rest you need and wake up refreshed for what matters most.

Developed as a sister app to [Prayer Shield](https://github.com/darkswich1234-ux/PrayerShield), it shares a unified design language and optional synchronization for Fajr prayer times.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Wind-Down Protection**: Automatically blocks distracting apps (Social Media, Streaming, Games) when it's time for bed.
- **Smart Wake Plans**: Choose between **Fajr**, **School**, or **Work** wake targets. The app calculates your optimal sleep window based on your goal.
- **Prayer Shield Sync**: Seamlessly import your Fajr time from Prayer Shield to ensure your sleep schedule supports your morning spiritual practice.
- **AMOLED Black Theme**: A minimalistic, pure black mode designed to reduce eye strain and save battery on OLED screens.
- **Robust Protection Suite**:
  - **Smarter App Sorter**: Automatically categorizes your installed apps for easy blocking.
  - **Uninstall Protection**: Optional device administrator mode to prevent accidental or impulsive removal.
  - **Settings Lock**: Prevents access to system settings while Sleep Mode is active.
- **Customizable Widgets**:
  - **1x1 Toggle**: Minimalistic button for quick activation.
  - **2x2 Status**: View your current mode and next wake-up time.
  - **4x2 Dashboard**: A full sleep overview directly on your home screen.
- **Native Alarm Engine**: reliable waking with high-priority notifications and optional sync to your device's native clock app.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites
- Android 7.0 (API 24) or higher.
- Fully native Kotlin & Jetpack Compose implementation.

### Installation
1. Download **Sleep Shield v1.apk** from the releases page.
2. Follow the onboarding flow to enable **Accessibility Services** (required to block apps).
3. (Optional) Enable **Uninstall Protection** for maximum discipline.
4. Link to **Prayer Shield** in the Settings tab for automatic Fajr time updates.
